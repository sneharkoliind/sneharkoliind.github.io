I've spent the last few years building production systems: Python backends, event-driven platforms on AWS, and lately agentic AI systems for document processing.

Most of what I learn ends up in design docs, pull requests and conversations that nobody outside the team sees. This blog is where I'll write some of it down, partly to make it useful for others and partly because explaining something is the best test of whether I understand it.

## What I'll write about

- **Distributed systems in practice:** outbox patterns, idempotency, retries, dead-letter queues, and the failure modes that only show up in production
- **AI platforms:** multi-agent orchestration, MCP, moving between agent runtimes, and keeping LLM costs and quality under control
- **Python backend engineering:** Flask, Django and FastAPI, async, testing, and the details that matter at scale
- **What I'm learning next**, including the areas I'm actively studying

If something here is useful, or wrong, I'd like to hear about it. You can reach me on LinkedIn.
